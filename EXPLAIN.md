# SaaS Inventory Management System - Product Overview

## Executive Summary

The **SaaS Inventory Management System** is a comprehensive, enterprise-grade web application designed to streamline inventory operations, procurement, and sales processes for modern businesses. Built with scalability and performance in mind, it offers a robust multi-tenant architecture that allows organizations to manage multiple warehouses, track stock in real-time, and gain actionable insights through dynamic dashboards.

Whether you are a small e-commerce business or a growing enterprise with complex logistics, this system provides the tools to optimize your supply chain and reduce operational overhead.

---

## Key Capabilities

### 📦 Advanced Inventory Control
- **Multi-Warehouse Management**: Track stock across multiple physical locations with ease.
- **Granular Location Tracking**: Define Aisles, Racks, Shelves, and Bins for precise item location.
- **Stock Movements**: Record Inbound, Outbound, Transfers, and Adjustments with full audit trails.
- **Product Variants & Bundles**: Support for complex product structures, including size/color variants and kitting (bundles).
- **Low Stock Alerts**: Automated notifications when stock dips below defined thresholds.

### 🛒 Procurement & Purchasing
- **Supplier Management**: Maintain a centralized database of suppliers with performance ratings.
- **Purchase Orders (PO)**: Full lifecycle management from Draft to Approval to Reception.
- **Partial Receiving**: Support for receiving partial shipments and tracking backorders.
- **Cost Tracking**: Monitor unit costs, shipping fees, and taxes for accurate margin analysis.

### 💰 Sales & CRM
- **Customer Database**: Track customer details, lifetime value (LTV), and order history.
- **Sales Orders (SO)**: Manage quotes and orders with status tracking (Draft, Confirmed, Shipped, Delivered).
- **Payment Tracking**: Monitor payment statuses (Pending, Paid, Partial, Refunded).
- **Profit Analysis**: Real-time calculation of profit margins per order.

### 📊 Intelligence & Analytics
- **Customizable Dashboards**: Drag-and-drop widgets to create personalized views for different roles.
- **Real-time Metrics**: Visualize sales trends, inventory turnover, and top-performing products.
- **Inventory Audits**: Tools to conduct physical stock counts and reconcile discrepancies.

### ⚙️ Automation & Customization
- **Automation Rules**: Set up "If This Then That" style rules (e.g., "If stock < 10, email Manager").
- **Custom Fields**: Add custom data fields to Products, Orders, or Customers to fit your specific business needs.
- **Feature Flags**: Toggle specific features on/off for different organizations or users.
